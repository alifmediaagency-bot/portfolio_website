FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I budget for Google Ads? +
The right budget depends on your industry, competition, and goals. As a general rule, I recommend starting with at least $1,000–$2,000/month in ad spend so there's enough data to optimize. We'll discuss the ideal budget during our discovery call based on your specific market.
How long before I see results? +
You can start seeing traffic within hours of launch. Meaningful optimization data typically comes within 30–60 days. Most clients see significant ROI improvements within 60–90 days as I tune the campaigns based on real performance data.
Do you require long-term contracts? +
I recommend a minimum 3-month commitment so campaigns have time to properly optimize and deliver results. After that, we move to a month-to-month arrangement. I believe in earning your business every month, not locking you in.
Will I own my Google Ads account? +
Always. Your account is 100% yours. I work inside your Google Ads account as a manager — all campaigns, keywords, and data belong to you. If we stop working together, everything stays with you.
What industries do you work with? +
I've managed successful campaigns for e-commerce, legal, healthcare, SaaS, home services, real estate, and education industries. As long as Google Ads is a viable channel for your business, I can likely help — we'll discuss fit on the discovery call.
What reporting do you provide? +
All clients receive a monthly performance report covering impressions, clicks, conversions, cost per conversion, ROAS, and trend analysis. Growth and Enterprise clients also get access to a real-time Looker Studio dashboard connected to their account.
